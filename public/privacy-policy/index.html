Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data protection are paramount to us

Effective Date: November 8, 2026  •  Version: 2.0

1. Information We Collect

To enable booking features and optimal routing, we collect several categories of information:

  • Personal Identity Data: Name, age, gender, contact number, email identity, and government IDs where legally required for ticket generation (e.g., IRCTC logs).
  • Location Metrics: High-precision GPS tracking coordinates to display dynamic route maps and guide real-time travels across Indian regions.
  • Financial Logs: Transaction tokens processed through encrypted standard third-party checkouts (we do not directly store credit/debit card numbers).

2. How We Use Data

Your gathered details systematically serve the following operational paths:

  • Issuing legitimate and verified booking confirmations for trains, buses, cars, bikes, and hotels.
  • Powering customized map paths, local geo-suggestions, and navigational routing.
  • Communicating emergency trip status alterations or security tracking parameters.

3. Data Sharing and Protection

We do not lease or monetize your private details to independent advertising networks. Your information strictly gets passed forward only to the chosen transport vendors, hotel properties, or statutory entities required to complete your booking. We implement industry-leading encryption parameters to protect active traffic streams.
